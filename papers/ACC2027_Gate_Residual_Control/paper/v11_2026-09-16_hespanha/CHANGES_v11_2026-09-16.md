## 3. Hardstep, per your instruction (b+c)

The settling-time block is **not** in the table. It is in the Sec. V-B text:

- Hardstep commands 63–111° rotations every 2 s, so **97–99% of its mean error is slew** no bounded
  residual can remove; settled error is already 3.4° (X2) and 0.7° (CF2).
- Scored by time to a 5° band, the residual cuts settling **1.24 → 0.86 s on X2 (−31%)** and
  **1.18 → 0.68 s on CF2 (−42%)** at ρ=1.
- At ρ=3 the loop outruns the 9.6 rad/s PES corner by 72° of phase while the stiffer baseline
  saturates on 14–45% (X2) and 69–94% (CF2) of samples. On a saturated axis the interaction is
  one-sided: an agreeing residual is clipped away, an opposing one subtracts up to βℓ. That is the
  only cell in the study where the residual degrades performance.
- The **saturation-gated residual** (sᵢ←0 when the axis is saturated and the residual opposes it)
  restores settling to 0.57 s (X2) and 0.51 s (CF2), below the PD baselines, and keeps β = 0.5
  admissible on CF2 where the ungated law diverges. The text states plainly that this was tested
  **only on the saturated Hardstep cells at ρ=3**, that a systematic study under noise, disturbance
  and mismatch is future work, and that **all reported results use the ungated law**.
