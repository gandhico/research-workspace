## Recommended contribution statement

We introduce and evaluate a specific paired continuous membrane-gate feature basis, with an online PES-adapted decoder and bounded additive quaternion-torque augmentation. The experiments distinguish intrinsic conductance modulation, decoder learning, and residual authority. A custom non-spiking Nengo realization numerically reproduces the direct implementation. The verified annealed activity-trace alternative belongs to a precursor implementation and is reported separately. Preliminary Hover tests support execution feasibility and a small repeated Roll benefit.

This is an architecture/integration/evaluation contribution. Bounded states, weights, and command deviation are defensible structural properties, but do not establish tracking stability or safe state trajectories. Whether the CMG basis is superior to simpler stateful features remains an open ablation question.

## Mathematics and signal interpretation

The current three-axis path is:

```text
MuJoCo physical Q, body omega
    -> q = conjugate(Q), desired passive q_d
    -> quaternion error e_q and shortest angle theta_q
    -> fixed quaternion PD -> clipped baseline torque u0
    -> normalization by torque limits -> six paired currents
    -> CMG gate explicit-Euler step
    -> CMG membrane frozen-coefficient exponential step
    -> activity a = clip(100000 r, 0, 1)
    -> s = clip(W_k a, -1, 1)
    -> requested additive torque clip(u0 + beta * limits * s)
    -> collective thrust + rotor allocator -> rotor clip -> MuJoCo

e_q, omega -> clipped teaching target y*
y* - s, a -> PES update -> leak + weight projection -> W_(k+1)
```

The command uses pre-update W. The gate state is not the decoder: r is a state, a is its readout/activity, and W is the decoder. Membrane voltage influences the gate target and the gate influences membrane conductance. The decoder does not read membrane directly in the canonical CMG experiment.

Three meanings of adaptation must remain distinct:

1. Intrinsic modulation: the chi*g1*abs(I) conductance term.
2. Online learning: changes to W through PES or AAT.
3. Authority selection: beta and actuator/residual caps; these are chosen parameters, not learned by PES.

Four restricted propositions are included in v5:

- Internal-state bounds from convex-combination updates and bounded currents.
- Requested-command bounds and componentwise baseline-deviation bound from clipping nonexpansiveness.
- Decoder boundedness by projection; constant-feature PES error contraction only when target/features are fixed and clipping, projection, and leak are inactive.
- AAT trace bounds and a frozen-regressor error identity; no coupled plant/trace convergence theorem.

Numerical algebra checks complement the derivations; they do not prove stability. Per-step eta and leak are explicitly distinguished from rates per second.

Actual AAT implementation:

```text
rho(t) = 0.98 - 0.48*t/T
z_k = rho*z_(k-1) + (1-rho)*a_k
eta_AAT(t) = 2*eta_PES*(1 - 0.8*t/T)
W_next = clip(W + eta_AAT*(target-s)*z^T - leak*W, -6, 6)
```

At 0.5 ms, frozen-rho trace time constant decreases from 24.75 ms to 0.72 ms. Starting learning rate is twice PES; final rate is 0.4 times PES. This is an annealed activity trace, not the full state-sensitivity e-prop of Bellec et al. Separate trace-only, schedule-only, and combined tests are needed to attribute any benefit.

## What should happen before submission

1. Locate the seven missing annealing result files, or keep the three-row precursor table. Do not restore untraceable values by copying them from v4.
2. Run a matched additive PES/AAT factorial comparison if AAT is to become a central validated contribution rather than an exploratory subsection. No such runs were performed in this audit.
3. Add a stronger classical baseline (desired-rate feedforward or geometric tracking) and a simple adaptive residual feature baseline. The current gains may compensate phase/amplitude shortcomings of the fixed feedforward-free PD.
4. Calibrate neuron operating points and compare actual correction effort, not merely identical caps. Add frozen-weight/held-out tests before claiming learned generalization.
5. For robustness claims, collect replicated noisy/disturbed trials and hardware perturbation tests. Nominal deterministic cases do not establish them.
6. Finalize human author names/affiliations, funding, any required tool-use disclosure, official ACC template, and conference page limit. The current IEEEtran PDF is a reading draft, not a certified submission-format file.
7. Keep all five primary missions in the paper table. Favorable illustrative plots may be selected, but the selection should not conceal available adverse comparisons or different metrics.
