## What the Izhikevich numbers rest on (and their limits)

* 33 runs, ~9 min: nominal five missions x two airframes (gated), Hardstep ungated, realistic noise seeds 1-3 on Roll/Hard45/Hardstep, hover bursts 1x. **Reduced grid**, declared before the runs (PREREGISTRATION section 10); what was not run is listed in the report.
* Validation against the archived runner: X2 bit-exact; **CF2 missed the declared 1e-9 tolerance (3.05e-4)** because the two runners differ by ~5e-20 N m in the residual arithmetic, which the chaotic spiking cell amplifies (a 1e-14 rad initial offset changes a CF2 mean by 0.5 %). Amendment A3.1.
* Single-run scatter of the spiking cell measured by a 24-run ensemble: s.d. 1.4 % of the error at worst; differences below 4 points of the PD+ff error are not interpreted (A3.2). Full report: `REPORT_IZHIKEVICH_A3.md`.
