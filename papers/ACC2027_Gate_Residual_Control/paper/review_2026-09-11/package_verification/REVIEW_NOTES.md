## What the new proof does

Theorem 1 is a **conditional local input-to-state/practical-stability result for constant-reference attitude regulation**. It is an application of standard Lyapunov reasoning, not a claim of a new global quaternion-control theorem.

1. Construct the local plant state x=(e_q, omega) on a smooth quaternion sign branch.
2. Keep the actual positive diagonal K_q and K_omega and the physical (possibly non-diagonal) J. The local linear matrix A is Hurwitz; no scalar-gain substitution or gyroscopic cancellation is introduced.
3. Solve `A^T P + P A = -Q_L` and bound the nonlinear remainder by `L_r ||x||^2` locally.
4. Aggregate residual torque, baseline clipping/allocation mismatch, passive torque, and external disturbances into the **realized** torque deviation d.
5. For sufficiently small initial state and total perturbation, prove forward invariance of a Lyapunov ellipsoid and `V(t) <= exp(-alpha t)V(0) + C dbar^2`.
6. Bound the CMG contribution after allocation by `L_A beta ||ell||` for the same state and collective thrust. This is independent of what the decoder learned.

The theorem establishes local exponential convergence only when the total perturbation vanishes. With nonzero bounded perturbation, it gives an ultimate bound, not zero tracking error. It does not establish decoder convergence or identification.

### Numerical checks and limits

| Check | CF2 | X2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Largest real part of local A eigenvalues [1/s] | -3.325 | -1.39518 |
| Lyapunov equation residual norm (Q_L=I) | 2.48e-16 | 4.50e-16 |
| Post-allocation Euclidean gain bound L_A | 3.326923 | 4.533445 |
| Illustrative conservative sufficient total disturbance bound [N m] | <4.26e-7 | <1.70e-4 |
| Uniform allocation bound at beta=.5 [N m] | 8.64e-4 | 2.01 |

The last two rows emphatically **do not certify the experimental beta=.5**. The uniform worst-case bound is much larger than this illustrative local sufficient threshold. That failure of a conservative certificate does not prove instability; it means the proof cannot be advertised as covering these runs. The norm-based radius calculation and matrices are in `review_checks.json`.

The proof also does not cover changing reference rates/accelerations, crossing the 180-degree sign boundary, full translation/altitude stability, hardware actuator lag, or the exact discrete MuJoCo/Nengo interconnection. Sampling error would require a separate bound or a discrete-time/hybrid Lyapunov argument. Do not cite nominal simulations as proof that these assumptions hold globally.

## How the advisor's guide influenced the revision

The guide is Joao P. Hespanha's *Writing a Control Paper*. Applied principles: a self-contained abstract stating problem/method/result; literature positioned before contributions; plant, inputs, measurements, and objective stated before architecture; main result with explicit hypotheses and proof; figures called out with what they establish; and conclusions separating findings from open work. The guide's warnings about unmatched benchmarks, single stochastic runs, and selectively favorable examples particularly motivated the noise, family-comparison, and retention qualifications.

## Recommended next work, in priority order

1. **Classical comparator:** add a documented PID/integral or geometric baseline with equivalent actuator restrictions, and fair tuning effort. Current improvements are against a fixed PD law with no desired-rate feedforward or integral action.
2. **Noise scoring:** retain an uncorrupted ground-truth quaternion/error alongside corrupted feedback, use more paired seeds, report absolute PD and CMG errors as well as reductions. Do not overwrite the old campaign.
3. **Neuron fairness:** run a separate normalized-update benchmark using the correct S_f-squared scaling if equal normalized eta is the objective; also consider matched realized effort and comparable feature bandwidth. Keep current figures labeled exploratory until replaced.
4. **CMG necessity:** compare fixed/learned simple bounded bases, gate gain G, membrane vs gate readout, and a non-neural leaky basis. Conductance insensitivity currently weakens a neuron-specific mechanism claim.
5. **Retention:** compare persistent weights, decoder reset at each burst, and frozen learned weights on identical phase-reset disturbances. Present performance in quiet intervals, including negative results.
6. **Stability:** have coauthors check the local theorem first. A mission-wide theorem needs reference dynamics, actuator feasibility/headroom, model uncertainty, and sampled-data treatment; do not add a global convergence claim to the present PES rule.
