## 1. Ablation strengthened (Sec. V-D)

The section now traces the gain row by row through Fig. 5, with every number
verified against the 2-kHz neural archives of the matched neuron-family
campaign (`results/neuron_family_matched_45s/`):

- **Fixed decoder helps, but little.** X2 0.12-10.27%, CF2 0.06-6.80%; at most
  a fifth of the PES gain in any cell.
- **Membrane/gate.** Rest is `v ~ 0.75 < v_th`, so
  `r = sigma(-12.5) ~ 3.7e-6`: the gate sits in its logistic tail, not closed.
- **Latching.** `r >= 1/2` with `gamma = 2` and `v >= 0` (Prop. 1) forces
  `b >= 1/2`, so an opened gate cannot close. No gate reclosed in any of the
  twenty runs.
- **Pair cancellation.** With the fixed decoder only the +-y pair latches on
  Full3 (both models), so `a_y^+ - a_y^- = 0` and the pitch residual is
  *exactly* zero after 1 s.
- **PES.** On X2 Full3 `a = 1` from 2.02 s (all gates latched by 2.4 s), so the
  decoder alone carries the correction; the constant-feature recursion (33) is
  the leaky regime of Prop. 3, pole 0.9952 (~0.10 s).
- **Effort.** Residual RMS 0.026 -> 0.123 N m, decoder drift 0.37; PES spends
  4-12x the fixed residual RMS on Roll/Full3/Hard45/Hardstep.
- **Where no gate opens** (Roll, Yaw360; `r <= 1.6e-3`), PES lifts the membranes
  toward threshold (`v <= 0.87`) and uses the graded tail.

Fig. 5's caption now names all five rows and the log gate axis. The abstract,
