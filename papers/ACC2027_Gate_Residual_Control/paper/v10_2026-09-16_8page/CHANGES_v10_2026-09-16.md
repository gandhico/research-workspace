## 1. Why this revision

The PD of eq. (11) damps absolute body rate, while eq. (6) of the same paper defines the rate error
`ω_e = ω − R(q_e)ᵀω_d`. A reviewer who reads those two equations together will ask for the feedforward
baseline. We measured it (2026-09-10/11) and the answer is partly unfavourable, so v10 reports it rather
than waiting to be asked.

**The three readings, as printed.** (i) The residual improves every rung, by a shrinking margin: 44.5/44.2 %
over PD, 57.8/57.6 % over PD+ff at ρ = 1, 35.6/6.5 % at ρ = 3 (X2/CF2). (ii) A tuned feedforward PD alone
beats the PD-referenced augmented controller in every cell — 13–300× on smooth missions, .266 vs .359 rad
on X2 Hardstep. (iii) At ρ = 3 the residual *degrades* Hardstep by 17.0 % (X2) and 1.3 % (CF2).

Also stated: β was selected per model by a rule fixed before the runs (X2 0.5, CF2 0.05, because β ≥ 0.1
diverges on CF2 Hardstep), and realized residual effort falls as the baseline improves
(.121 → .0216 → .0148 N·m on X2 Full3).
