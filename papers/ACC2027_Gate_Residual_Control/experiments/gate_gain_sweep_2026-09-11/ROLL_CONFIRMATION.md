# Completion of the initially partial Roll sensitivity curve

The initial Roll batch found strong dependence on readout gain: about zero improvement at G=1 versus 17.36% at G=1e5. Two additional paired chi cases at G=1e6 complete the originally specified six-gain Roll range. The expected direction is motivated by activity amplification and the reduced PES model, but no numerical outcome was assumed. These are follow-up sensitivity tests, not independent validation of an optimized controller. The frozen main matrix remains at G=1e5.
