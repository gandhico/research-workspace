# 6-DOF cascade: outer-loop feedforward, re-tuning, and CMG on top

2026-09-10. MuJoCo, 15 s missions, dt = 0.5 ms, noise-free, one run per
cell (deterministic). Mean position error [m] / mean attitude error [°].

## Headline

1. **The canonical 6-DOF baselines were under-tuned, and the outer loop had
   no feedforward.** The inner attitude loop already damped `ω − ω_des`; the
   position loop damped absolute velocity, `F0 = m g e_z − k_p(p − p_ref) − k_d v`.
   Adding flatness feedforward, `m(g e_z + a_ref) − k_p(p − p_ref) − k_d(v − v_ref)`,
   and re-tuning both loops cuts moving-reference position error by
   **98–99%** on both airframes.
2. **CMG–PES still helps on top of the tuned cascade, modestly:** 13–25% lower
   position error on Circle and Full6, at a small cost on Waypoint (+3.5% to
   +7%). On CF2 attitude is essentially unchanged; on X2 it rises ~20%.
3. **The old 6-DOF CMG numbers are against the under-tuned baseline** (e.g. X2
   Full6 0.99 → 0.24 m). They should be restated against the tuned cascade.

## Why feedforward first made X2 worse

At the canonical `wn_att = 4`, adding position feedforward made the classical
X2 cascade *worse* (Circle 0.88 → 1.38 m). The tilt demands arrived faster
than the slow attitude loop could follow. At `wn_att = 6` plain PD already
drops Circle to 0.41 m and attitude error from 13.9° to 0.6°, and with
feedforward at `wn_att = 8` it reaches 0.060 m. The progress note describing
`wn_att = 4` as X2's "practical ceiling" does not hold on these missions: with
`wn_pos = [1, 1, 1.2]`, attitude bandwidths from 6 to 24 rad/s are stable.

## How the configurations were chosen

The rule was fixed in `compile_ff6_report.py` before the final results:

- **baseline**: the `(wn_att, wn_pos)` minimising
  `pos(waypoint) + pos(circle) + pos(full6)` among stable runs
  (every mission pos < 1 m, att < 20°). Hover is excluded — every stable
  configuration holds it to within millimetres.
- **CMG β**: the same score, for CMG on the selected baseline.

Every choice is an **interior optimum**: X2 `wn_att` 16/20/24 and
`wn_pos` 1.5/2/2.5 are all worse than or equal to the selection;
CF2 `wn_att` 20/24/28 and `wn_pos` 2/2.25/2.5 likewise; X2 β = 0.05 sits
inside 0.008–0.1, CF2 β = 0.008 inside 0.002–0.05. The X2 surface is flat
across `wn_att` 16–24 (scores 0.049–0.054).

Tuning on the evaluation missions favours the baseline, which is the
conservative direction for the CMG claim.

## Integrity

- `run_6dof_cascade.py` is untouched. The variant `run_6dof_cascade_ff.py` is
  generated by `patch_6dof_ff.py`; with `--pos-ff none` it reproduces the X2
  `optionA` runs and all eight CF2 `optionA_cf2_tuned` runs **bit-for-bit**.
- `v_ref` and `a_ref` are central finite differences of `position_reference`.
  On Waypoint's jumps a sample straddling a step would feed an impulse
  forward; such samples get zero feedforward. Hover and Waypoint are therefore
  unaffected by feedforward, as they should be.

## Caveats

- **At the tuned gains, X2 no longer holds hover perfectly still**
  (1.3 mm, 0.12°, where the canonical loop sits at exactly zero), which
  suggests a small limit cycle at high bandwidth. The selection score excludes
  hover, so it did not penalise this.
- Noise-free and deterministic. Higher bandwidth is rewarded until something
  saturates; under sensor noise the optimum would sit lower. The attitude-only
  study is testing exactly this for PD + feedforward.
- 15 s missions, as in the canonical 6-DOF runs.
- CMG costs something on Waypoint on both airframes; the gain is on smooth
  moving references.
