## Interpretation (written after compiling, 2026-09-12)

### Claims under the declared rules

**C1 holds.** Attitude-priority allocation leaves both baselines unchanged: the error ratio against clip is 0.998–1.000 on both vehicles.

**C2 fails on CF2.**
- The ungated target tuner *is* admissible on all 9 missions on both vehicles. On CF2, attitude priority removes its crash and cuts its error by 22% against clip (0.776).
- The ungated dual still crashes on CF2 Hardstep (4.88× PID).
- On X2's new held-out Hardstep_c, the ungated dual stays airborne but reaches 2.56× PID.
- Attitude priority fixes the single tuner but not the ungated dual, so gating stays necessary for the dual.

**C3 fails.** The ungated dual is worse than the residual alone over all 9 missions: 1.057 (X2), 1.152 (CF2).

**C4 holds, but the effect is small.**
- (a) The dither sensitivity is positive on every axis in every smooth-mission cell, on both vehicles. The sign bias of the published law (§6c) is removed.
- (b) stn_dx_v3 vs PID on smooth missions: 0.982 (X2), 0.954 (CF2).
- (c) dual_stn_dx_v3 vs the residual over all 9 missions: 0.995 (X2), 0.988 (CF2).
- Why small: the PES estimator learns Ŷ ≈ 0.2–0.4 on tracking missions (it averages a clipped target), so the gains move by only about ±5%.

dual_v2 is admissible everywhere on both vehicles, under both the clip and apa allocations. It is the most defensible dual controller so far: consistent but modest.

### Key finding: the dither identifies the X2 axis-inertia mismatch quantitatively

- X2 lock-in estimates: ŝ ≈ (1.58, 1.38, 0.42) on smooth missions.
- Theory (§7.2) predicts ŝ = j_tune / J_true per axis.
- From the model: j_tune / diag(J) = (1.656, 1.435, 0.420). X2's principal axes are permuted relative to its body axes by `body_iquat`.
- CF2: ŝ ≈ 0.98, and j_tune / diag(J) = 1.00.

**The identification is correct in magnitude, not only in sign.** It also reveals that the X2 PD gains (ACC 2027 eq. 11 and every controller here) are **1.66× stiffer than physically scaled on roll, 1.43× on pitch, and 0.42× (too soft) on yaw.** That is consistent with the large residual gains on X2 Yaw360.

### Next (v4, to be declared before any run)

This uses the magnitude the dither already delivers, instead of the gradient's weak signal.
