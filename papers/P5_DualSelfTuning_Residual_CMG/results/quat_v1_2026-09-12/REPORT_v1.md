## Verdicts and interpretation (written after compiling, 2026-09-12)

1. **Faithful CMG STN-PID-PES is inadmissible on both vehicles.** It reaches 1.6–8.5× the PID error on tracking missions.
   - The gain multipliers go negative (m_p down to −4).
   - The learned sensitivity is negative in 41–77% of steps on tracking missions, as derived in ARCHITECTURE_MATH §6c (closed loop gives −2/k_q).
   - It helps only on Yaw360, where Ŷ stays positive (+0.5 to +0.7).
   - The published law does not transfer to attitude control as written.
2. **Bounding makes it safe but not useful.** stn_b is admissible everywhere, with geometric mean 1.10 (X2) and 1.15 (CF2); held-out 1.15 and 1.18 agree. The wrong sign de-tunes stiffness (m_p about 0.62–0.77).
3. **With the known sign, self-tuning helps where authority allows.** On X2, stn_b_ks reaches 0.758 (original) and 0.767 (held-out), slightly better than the target tuner (0.79). On CF2 it crashes on both step missions.
4. **Dual versus residual alone (H2) holds on smooth missions but not on steps.**
   - Geometric mean without steps: dual 0.489 (X2) and 0.490 (CF2) vs pid_res 0.544 and 0.545. Held-out Hard45b: 0.389 and 0.379 vs 0.454 and 0.449. This is consistent across both vehicles and both mission sets.
   - With steps included, X2 held-out Hardstep_b is 1.03–1.10 (dual) vs 0.87 (pid_res), so the direction-agreement rule fails.
   - H2 is supported only for smooth tracking.
5. **The residual alone (ACC 2027) remains the most robust augmentation.** It is admissible in every cell, on both vehicles, original and held-out.

**Mechanism.**
- Each 1.9-rad step costs altitude, even for PID (z dips to about 0.65–0.74 m).
- More aggressive attitude responses, from raised stiffness or residual + tuner interplay, take rotor thrust from the collective loop. The clipped inverse allocation has no thrust priority, and T/W is 1.98.
- Repeated transients end in ground contact.
- Saturation flags arrive too late to prevent this, which is why the freeze did not help.
- De-tuned controllers survive.

**Rule gap.** The quaternion-only admissibility rule misses altitude loss. v2 adds "no ground contact (z_min > 0.2 m)".
