## Interpretation (written after the results; the tables above are generated and unchanged)

Figure: `v4_figure.png` / `.pdf` (`experiments/quat_dual_v0/plot_v4.py`).

1. **Physically scaled gains matter little at this bandwidth (D2).**
   - On X2, `pid_body` is only 3.2% better than `pid` (geometric mean 0.968), even though the X2 gains are mis-scaled by
     ×1.66 / ×1.44 / ×0.42.
   - On CF2 it is identical (G7).
2. **The ACC 2027 residual gain is not a compensation artifact (D5).**
   - On X2 the residual lowers error to 0.641× on the body-axis PID, versus 0.613× on the j_tune PID, and
     `pid_res_body` / `pid_res` = 1.012.
   - Almost all of the X2 residual improvement survives correct gain scaling. This is useful for the paper: the
     mis-scaling note is a disclosure, not a threat to the claim.
3. **The identification is right in mean, but the gain law built on it fails (D1 and D3 fail).**
   - Mean ŝ matches the model on every smooth mission: X2 1.58–1.62 / 1.38–1.41 / 0.41–0.42; CF2 0.96–0.98.
   - On single-axis missions the multipliers converge to the model targets: X2 roll mission (0.66, 0.70, 2.36);
     CF2 roll mission (1.00, 1.01, 1.00).
   - On multi-axis missions every axis drifts down to 0.5–0.75, including axes whose target is 1 or 2.38.
   - Diagnosis:
     - the instantaneous ŝ is noisy (for `idgc_v4` on multi-axis missions, ŝ ≤ 0 on 16–47% of post-warm-up steps per
       axis, because tracking motion leaks through the τ_d = 0.5 s lock-in);
     - the law inverts that instantaneous value;
     - increases are headroom-scaled (h < 1) while decreases are not, so the noise is rectified into a downward drift.
   - Result: `idgc_v4` is 5.6% worse than PID on X2 and 2.2% worse on CF2.
4. **The residual contaminates the identification (D4 fails).**
   - `dual_idgc_v4` has the best X2 geometric mean in the study: 0.595 vs PID, 0.969 vs `pid_res`, 0.993 vs
     `dual_v2`.
   - But X2 Hardstep_c reaches 1.27 and CF2 Hardstep crashes.
   - The residual is extra feedback the estimator does not model: ŝ = −12 on CF2 Hardstep and −0.28 on X2 Yaw360
     roll. This is the same closed-loop mechanism quantified in the 6-DOF pilot.
5. **v5 directions (not declared):**
   - estimate first, then invert: slow second-stage average of ŝ (or of the complex response) before inversion;
   - symmetric updates in log m, with headroom acting on the applied gain rather than on the update direction;
   - an excitation / consistency validity test (see the known-controller check in the 6-DOF pilot);
   - freeze identification while the residual is active, or include the residual in the loop model.
