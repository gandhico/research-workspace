## Verdicts under the pre-declared rules

**H1 (the self-tuned PID beats PID).**
- X2: supported, with a geometric mean of 0.791 and improvement on every mission.
- CF2: **not admissible**. Hardstep diverged: 2.11 rad, rotor saturation 0.80.

**H2 (dual beats PID + residual).**
- X2: supported, 0.540 against 0.585. Dual is better on Roll, Full3, Hard45 and Yaw360 (dual/pid_res 0.85–0.96), and 2.2% worse on Hardstep.
- CF2: **not admissible**. Dual on Hardstep diverged: 2.45 rad, saturation 0.96.
- CF2 without Hardstep (reported separately, not ranked): dual 0.490 against pid_res 0.545.

**Unchanged from ACC 2027.** The residual alone (pid_res) is admissible everywhere, with geometric means of 0.585 (X2) and 0.590 (CF2).

## Diagnosis (hypothesis, not yet tested)

On CF2 Hardstep the tuner pushes k_p and k_i toward the top of their box (mean multipliers 1.37–1.48) while the error is large.

The v0 targets `tanh(|e|/e_ref)` saturate at 1 for step-sized errors. The tuner therefore raises stiffness and integral action exactly when the actuators are saturated. That is a gain-windup mechanism, analogous to the high-gain PID divergence seen on CF2 Hardstep in the 2026-09-10 baseline study.

X2, with its thrust-to-weight ratio of 4.0 against CF2's 1.98, has enough authority to absorb it.

## Next (v1, exploratory, needs a held-out check under rule 5)

2. Cap the target by the saturation margin, `y*_p <= 1 - |u0|/l`.
3. Re-run all 40 cells and add held-out missions (not used for the fix): a new multi-sine, and Hardstep with a different step sequence.
