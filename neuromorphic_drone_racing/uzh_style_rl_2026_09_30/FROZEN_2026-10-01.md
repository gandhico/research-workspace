# CMG-RL drone racing, X2, state-based (stage S1) — FROZEN 2026-10-01

Status: frozen at Gandhi's request. Nothing in `results/` is edited after this point; `results/FROZEN_SUMMARY.json` holds
the SHA-256 of the 14 source files and 64 run outputs. Tables: `results/FROZEN_SUMMARY.md` (built by `summarize_runs.py`).
Exploratory research line, **not** part of the ACC 2027 paper. Every result is **one seed** (seed 1).

## 1. Setup

- Task: Song et al. (IROS 2021) UZH recipe on our 7-gate MuJoCo track (Swift-format approximation, includes a split-S,
  gates 3→4), Skydio X2, perfect state, action = collective thrust + body rates (inner rate loop + rotor allocation).
- Training: PPO, 2×128 tanh actor/critic, 1024 parallel drones, 20M decisions per run, batched torch simulator
  (`vec_race.py`) validated against MuJoCo (one-decision error 0.003 mm / 0.007°; `results/s0_validation.json`).
- Transfer test: frozen policy exported to NumPy and flown in the full MuJoCo race world (`mujoco_eval.py`).

### How CMG enters the RL policy (feature-bank architecture)

```text
o_t (31-D state: body velocity, attitude, body rates, next two gates, previous action)
  ├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  └─► fixed random encoder (frozen, not trained) ─► 32 neuron currents J
         ─► neuron bank stepped over the 20-ms decision (CMG: 40 × 0.5 ms; HyperLIF: 200 × 0.1 ms)
         ─► 32 features f_t (CMG: clip(1e5·r); HyperLIF: 20-ms synaptic spike rate / 75 Hz)
                                                                  ▼
                       x_t = RunningNorm([o_t, f_t]) (63-D) ─► PPO actor/critic (2×128 tanh) ─► a_t
```
The neurons are a fixed recurrent temporal feature bank ("reservoir"): their state carries over between decisions
(reset only at episode start) and PPO learns only the readout networks. No PES, no gradient through the neurons. The
policy always also sees the raw state, so the neurons can only add information.

Arms (32 units each, identical PPO):
| arm | line | neuron model | parity check |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mlp` | control | none (features = 0) | — |
| `cmg_acc` | non-spiking | exact ACC 2027 CMG (`gate.py` bank), paired currents J± = 0.75 + 1.25·max(±tanh(Eo), 0) | 1e-14 vs `gate.py` |
| `hyperlif_spike` | spiking | P2 HyperLIF (gate-conductance spiking, no hard reset), J ∈ [1.0, 2.25] | 8e-14 vs P2 neuron; f–I 13/14 |

## 2. Reward history (the main engineering result)

| version | reward | outcome (MLP) |
|---|---|---|
| v1 | progress = projection on gate-to-gate line (Song 2021) | reward hacking: 40 m/s dash out of arena |
| v2 | progress = decrease of distance to next gate centre | hover trap: survive, 0 gates |
| v3 | v2 + gate-pass bonus 10 | learns; stuck ~6M steps at split-S, first lap at 15.7M |
| v4 | v3 + 5-s stall termination | first lap at 9.8M, lap 9.42 s in MuJoCo |

## 3. Results (seed 1, 20M decisions)

Gates in a 15-s window, 512 episodes, deterministic policy (`eval_gap.json`); "random start" = UZH distributed
initialization along the track, the general skill; "official start" = the race start.

| run | random start: gates / crash | official start | MuJoCo (official start, 40 s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| mlp v4 | **7.51** / 24% | 10.0 gates, 100% laps | **lap 9.42 s, 0 collisions** |
| mlp v3 | 3.86 / 27% | 6.0 | lap 14.98 s, 0 collisions |
| hyperlif_spike v4 | **3.01** / 57% | 0.0 (hovers) | 0 gates, time limit |
| hyperlif_spike v3 | 2.48 / 22% | 3.0 | 3 gates, time limit |
| cmg_acc v3 | 0.84 / 79% | 3.0 | 3 gates, time limit |
| cmg_acc v4 | 0.80 / 98% | 2.0 (crash) | 2 gates, collision |

Batched-sim and MuJoCo gate times agree within 0.02–0.08 s for every policy flown.

Neuron statistics at the end of training: CMG features 81–90% saturated, 15–26% of units ever cross threshold (latch
regime); HyperLIF 53 Hz per unit, 93–96% of units active, 28% saturated, 18–19% silent (P2 spiking window).

## 4. Conclusions (seed 1; to be confirmed with seeds 2–3)

1. The UZH recipe works on our track and transfers sim-to-sim: the MLP control laps in full MuJoCo (9.42 s, 0 collisions).
2. Reward design dominated: three failures (hacking, hover trap, split-S stall) were reward problems, not network problems.
3. **Spiking line > non-spiking line in this RL setting.** From random starts HyperLIF reaches 2.5–3.0 gates vs 0.8 for
   exact-ACC CMG. Both are below the MLP (3.9–7.5).
4. The exact-ACC CMG latches (81–90% of features saturated): good for its paper role (a fast switch feeding a PES
   residual) but a nearly binary, low-information feature bank for RL. Under v4 it got *worse* (crash 98%).
5. HyperLIF v4's 0-gate official-start result is a start-state coverage failure (official start = 25% of training
   episodes), not an inability to race: it passes 3 gates/episode from random starts.

## 5. Flags

- One seed per arm; no confidence intervals. S1 acceptance (3/3 seeds lap in MuJoCo) met by none of the neuron arms.
- The neuron arms were not retuned (encoder gains, unit count, readout, CMG gain G); the comparison is "as specified",
  not "each at its best".
- The neurons add features to the full state; this tests whether they *help*, not whether they can *replace* the state.
- Archived and not comparable: v1 runs (`results/archive_v1_reward/`), Codex's non-coherent `cmg_spike` reset variant,
  the Codex-retuned `cmg` preset (no v3/v4 run), `hyperlif_gate` (never run).
- Training-curve evaluation used one deterministic official-start flight per 10 updates; `eval_gap.json` is the reliable
  measure.
